SPARKRAIL

— NEON ENERGY PUZZLE —

Sparkrail has NO lives or energy systems, NO timers, and is fully playable offline.

A relaxing neon colour-sorting puzzle for Android. Move glowing energy crystals through the relays at the ends of each circuit until every colour has its own circuit – and the whole grid lights up.

  • 120 hand-picked puzzles in six neon zones, each guaranteed solvable
  • Easy to learn: tap a crystal at the end of a circuit, tap where it should go
  • Free undo and tips, no timers, no lives, fully offline
  • Every colour has its own crystal shape (heart, star, flower …), English & German
