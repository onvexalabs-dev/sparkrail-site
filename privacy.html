Privacy Policy

Sparkrail (Android) · Effective: 21 September 2026

Short version: the game has no user accounts, no own servers and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device. The only third-party data processing happens through Google: rewarded video ads (Google AdMob, only if you have not bought Pro) and in-app purchases (Google Play). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice in the game at any time.

No data protection officer is appointed (not legally required).

2. Data stored only on your device

The game saves your progress (solved puzzles, stars, move counts), Sparks, badges, unlocked styles, settings (language, volume, vibration, accessibility), whether the tutorial was completed and whether Pro is active, in a file inside the app’s private storage. This data is never transmitted to us. Android cloud backup of this file is disabled. You can erase it at any time via Settings ▸ Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

3. Rewarded ads – Google AdMob

Current version: the game shows no ads and does not activate any ad SDK. This section describes how data will be processed once optional ads are introduced in a future update (the Google Play listing will then state “Contains ads”).

If you have not purchased Pro, the app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Ads are shown only when you actively choose to watch a video (e.g. for optional in-game extras). There are no banners, interstitials or app-open ads.

When the SDK is active, Google automatically processes:

  • IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived)
  • Device and advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID)
  • Information about app and ad interactions (e.g. app launch, video views, taps)
  • Diagnostic and performance information (e.g. launch time, energy use, crashes of the SDK)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, network type)

Purposes: delivering and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and security, and – only with your consent – personalised advertising.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before the ad SDK is started, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) asks for your choices. Your decision is stored on your device as a consent string. You can change or withdraw it at any time via Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021 for storage of and access to information on your device) and, for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security, our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

US state privacy laws: where applicable, the same form lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; additionally, Standard Contractual Clauses apply.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads or Google ▸ Ads). Information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

Pro users: after purchasing Pro, the ad SDK is no longer initialised and no ad requests are made.

4. In-app purchase – Google Play Billing

The optional one-time “Pro Upgrade” is sold exclusively through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we never receive your payment details. The app only receives a purchase confirmation (product ID, purchase token, status) from Google Play to unlock and restore Pro. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google Play’s own privacy policy applies to the store and payment.

5. Google Play distribution

Downloading the game from Google Play requires a Google account; Google processes the related data under its own responsibility. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying statistics from the Google Play Console (e.g. installs, crashes reported by Android’s system, ratings).

6. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the correspondence when your request is resolved, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and ensure security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website sets no cookies; a language preference may be stored in your browser’s local storage only when you press EN/DE.

8. What we do not do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no own servers, no analytics or tracking SDKs by us
  • No access to contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location
  • No sale of personal data by us

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating suitable for general audiences (PG).

10. Retention

On-device data stays until you reset progress or uninstall. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies. Emails: see section 6.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Because we hold no personal data on our own systems (except support emails), most requests concern data processed by Google; we will help you exercise them. Contact: onvexalabs@gmail.com.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Austria: Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

12. Changes

We will update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows the current version.

3. Belohnungswerbung – Google AdMob

Aktuelle Version: Das Spiel zeigt keine Werbung und aktiviert kein Werbe-SDK. Dieser Abschnitt beschreibt die Datenverarbeitung, sobald in einem künftigen Update freiwillige Werbung eingeführt wird (der Google-Play-Eintrag weist dann „Enthält Werbung“ aus).

Solange du Pro nicht gekauft hast, enthält die App das Google Mobile Ads SDK der Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland („Google“). Werbung wird nur angezeigt, wenn du aktiv ein Video auswählst (z. B. für optionale Extras im Spiel). Es gibt keine Banner, Interstitials oder App-Start-Werbung.

Wenn das SDK aktiv ist, verarbeitet Google automatisch:

  • IP-Adresse (daraus kann ein ungefährer Standort abgeleitet werden)
  • Geräte- und Werbe-IDs (Android-Werbe-ID, App-Set-ID)
  • Informationen über App- und Werbeinteraktionen (z. B. App-Start, Videoaufrufe, Tippen)
  • Diagnose- und Leistungsdaten (z. B. Startzeit, Energieverbrauch, Abstürze des SDK)
  • Geräteinformationen (Modell, Betriebssystemversion, Sprache, Netzwerktyp)

Zwecke: Auslieferung und Messung von Werbung, Frequenzbegrenzung, Betrugsprävention und Sicherheit sowie – nur mit deiner Einwilligung – personalisierte Werbung.

Einwilligung (EWR, UK, Schweiz): Bevor das Werbe-SDK gestartet wird, fragt ein Einwilligungsformular der User Messaging Platform von Google (eine zertifizierte Consent-Management-Plattform nach dem IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) deine Auswahl ab. Deine Entscheidung wird als Einwilligungs-String auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Du kannst sie jederzeit unter Einstellungen ▸ Werbe-Datenschutzoptionen ändern oder widerrufen. Ohne Einwilligung kann Google weiterhin nicht personalisierte bzw. eingeschränkte Werbung ausspielen. Rechtsgrundlage: deine Einwilligung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO; § 165 Abs. 3 TKG 2021 für das Speichern von und den Zugriff auf Informationen auf deinem Gerät) sowie für nicht personalisierte Werbung, Betrugsprävention und Sicherheit unser berechtigtes Interesse an der Finanzierung eines kostenlosen Spiels (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO).

US-Bundesstaaten: Soweit anwendbar, ermöglicht dasselbe Formular den Widerspruch gegen „Verkauf“ oder „Weitergabe“ personenbezogener Daten für zielgerichtete Werbung.

Übermittlung in Drittländer: Google kann Daten in den USA verarbeiten. Google LLC ist nach dem EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework zertifiziert; zusätzlich gelten Standardvertragsklauseln.

Deine Werbe-ID kannst du in den Android-Einstellungen zurücksetzen oder löschen (Einstellungen ▸ Datenschutz ▸ Werbung bzw. Google ▸ Werbung). Informationen: Wie Google Daten von Websites oder Apps nutzt, die Google-Dienste verwenden · Datenschutzerklärung von Google.
